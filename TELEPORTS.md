# Teleports

Copy a command from the tables below and paste it into Hytale chat. Case and spaces don't matter: `/azeroth Booty Bay` works too.

- In game, `/azeroth list <word>` searches the names (for example `/azeroth list grove`).
- The start of a name, or any part of it, works when only one place matches.
- When an instance and a place share a name, the instance wins. The place then needs `outside` at the end, as shown below (for example `/azeroth ulduar outside`).
- Instances are separate worlds. Use any continent command to get back.

## Portals

These have no command: walk through them.

| Portal | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Portal To Outland | Eastern Kingdoms | You step through the Dark Portal into Outland. |
| Dark Portal To Azeroth | Outland, Eversong and Azuremyst | You step back through the Dark Portal to Azeroth. |
| Karazhan gate | Deadwind Pass, Eastern Kingdoms | Walk in to enter Karazhan; walk out to leave. |
